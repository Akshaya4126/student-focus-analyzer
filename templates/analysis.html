How the estimate works

A Scikit-learn Random Forest classifier uses study duration, distraction duration, break duration, and your 1–10 concentration rating. It returns one of three categories: Low, Medium, or High focus. The model was trained and evaluated on a reproducible synthetic dataset, so its results are educational and should not be treated as a validated assessment of attention.
